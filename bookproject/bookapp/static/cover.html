Between the pages of forgotten books lies a library unlike any other. Its shelves hold countless lives, each one shaped by a different decision. When Mira discovers its hidden doors, she is given the chance to explore the paths she never took — but every story has a secret, and every choice comes with a price.

“Sometimes the smallest step can lead to the greatest adventure.” — A tale of hope, courage and second chances

About the author

A. K. Morgan writes stories about mysterious places, unforgettable journeys and the extraordinary magic hidden in everyday life. The Midnight Library is a celebration of imagination and the courage to begin again.
